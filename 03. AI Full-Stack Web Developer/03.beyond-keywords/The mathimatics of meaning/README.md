### The Mathematics of Meaning – Vectors & Embeddings

## 📖 Overview

This document introduces the mathematical concepts behind semantic search, vectors, and embeddings. It explains how computers can understand and compare the meaning of text rather than relying only on exact keyword matching.

## 💡 Key Takeaway

Embeddings bridge the gap between human language and mathematical computation. They allow computers to represent text as numerical vectors and compare meanings mathematically.

Semantic search uses these representations to retrieve relevant information based on meaning rather than relying only on exact keyword matches.
